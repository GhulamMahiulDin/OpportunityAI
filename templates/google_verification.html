Google Security & Verification Guide

When connecting your Google Account to OpportunityAI, you will see a warning screen saying "Google hasn't verified this app". This guide explains why this warning appears, how we protect your privacy, and the step-by-step instructions to proceed safely.

❓ Why does Google show this warning?

OpportunityAI scans your inbox for internship, fellowship, and scholarship emails, which requires the Gmail Read-Only permission (gmail.readonly).

Because this permission allows reading emails, Google classifies it as a Restricted Scope. Google requires all apps using restricted scopes to undergo a formal Cloud Application Security Assessment (CASA) Tier 2 conducted by external auditors, which costs between $4,500 and $15,000+ USD annually.

As an early-stage beta project, we cannot afford this audit fee yet. Therefore, Google displays their standard warning screen. This does not mean the app is unsafe; it simply means we have not completed the paid verification process.

🛡️ Our Privacy & Safety Commitments

✓ Read-Only Access

We only request permission to read emails. We cannot send, delete, or modify any messages in your inbox.

✓ High Security Encryption

Your access credentials and authorization tokens are encrypted using AES-256 standard and stored securely.

✓ Zero Data Selling

We never sell or share your data. Your email content is processed locally and only used to match you with career opportunities.

✓ Revoke Anytime

You can disconnect Gmail from your account settings at any time, or revoke access instantly via your Google Account permissions page.
